Privacy Policy - Ops Center Integration Test

Last updated: 2026-10-11

Who operates this app

"Ops Center Integration Test" is a private, single-operator integration test (TikTok Sandbox) operated by a single individual (the operator) to prepare videos and send them to the operator's own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data is processed

  • When the operator connects their own TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit, the app receives an access token and basic profile information (such as display name and avatar) for the permissions the operator approves.
  • Video files and captions that the operator chooses to send to their own TikTok inbox.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to perform the actions the operator requests: sending videos to the operator's own TikTok inbox and reading basic account information. Authentication credentials are stored locally in the operator-controlled Postiz/Ops Center environment. The app does not sell or disclose personal data to third parties, and does not collect data from other TikTok users. Content selected by the operator is transmitted to TikTok only when the operator requests that action. This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking.

Retention and deletion

The operator can disconnect the app at any time in TikTok's settings or in the app, which removes the stored token. Locally stored files can be deleted by the operator at any time.
