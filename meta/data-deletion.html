Data deletion instructions - Ops Center Integration Test

Last updated: 2026-10-12

"Ops Center Integration Test" is a private test integration (see the privacy policy). This page explains how to revoke its access and have the data it holds about you deleted.

Step 1 - Revoke the app's access on Facebook

  1. Sign in to Facebook with the account that connected the app.
  2. Open your Facebook settings and find Business integrations.
  3. Select Ops Center Integration Test and choose Remove.

Removing the app stops it from accessing your account and Pages. Do not tick any option that asks to delete posts you have already published unless you want those posts removed from Facebook.

Step 2 - Ask us to delete the data we hold

Send an email to joannaolivelive@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request - Ops Center Integration Test". Please include the name of the Facebook account and the Page(s) you connected so we can identify the records.

What will be deleted

  • the access tokens stored for your account and Pages;
  • the stored identifiers and profile information of your connected Pages;
  • the publishing content, publication status and metrics records the integration holds for those Pages.

We will act on the request promptly and confirm by email when the deletion is complete.

What is not covered

Posts and media already published to a Facebook Page remain on Facebook and are controlled by that Page's administrators and by Facebook. Page administrators can delete them on Facebook.
