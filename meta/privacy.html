Privacy Policy - Ops Center Integration Test

Last updated: 2026-10-12

What this app is

"Ops Center Integration Test" is a private test integration operated by a single individual (the operator). It is used to connect a Facebook Page that the operator administers to a publishing tool, so the operator can publish posts and media to that Page and read basic Page performance metrics. It is not a public product. During this testing phase it is used only by the operator and by people who have been given a role on the app.

Data the app handles

When the operator logs in with Facebook and selects a Page, the app receives, for the permissions the operator approves:

  • an access token for the operator's Facebook account and for the selected Page, and identifiers and basic profile information of the Pages the operator selects (for example Page name, Page ID and picture);
  • the content the operator chooses to publish to the Page (text, images, videos) and the status of those publications;
  • Page performance metrics (Page insights) that the operator's permissions allow the app to read.

The permissions requested are those needed for this purpose: listing the operator's Pages, publishing and managing posts and comments on the selected Page, reading Page content and engagement, reading Page insights, and Business Manager access to the Page's business assets.

Who processes the data

Publishing is performed by Postiz, an open-source publishing tool that the operator runs on the operator's own computer. Postiz processes the data listed above (tokens, content, publication status and metrics) as necessary to publish to Facebook and to show results to the operator. Meta (Facebook) processes data as described in Meta's own policies when the app publishes to or reads from a Page. We do not sell personal data and we do not share it with other third parties.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • This test integration does not run advertising or tracking, and this web page does not use cookies or analytics.
  • We do not collect data about people other than the account holder and the Pages they connect.

Storage and retention

Tokens, Page identifiers, publishing content and status are stored in the operator-controlled Postiz environment. They are kept while the Page remains connected and are removed when the connection is removed or when deletion is requested (see below). Content already published to a Facebook Page remains on Facebook and is controlled by that Page's administrators and Facebook.

Revoking access and requesting deletion

You can revoke the app's access at any time in your Facebook settings (Business integrations) by removing "Ops Center Integration Test". You can also ask for the data we hold about your account to be deleted by following the steps on the data deletion instructions page.

Changes

If this test integration changes materially (for example if it is made available to other people), this policy will be updated before that happens.
